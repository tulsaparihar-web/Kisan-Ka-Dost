## Editorial and safety rules

- Keep Hindi and English guidance equivalent.
- Identify breeds by official registered names/home tracts; do not infer breed purity from appearance alone.
- Feed and ration guidance must remain qualitative until validated ration standards and required animal inputs are available.
- Do not publish universal daily feed quantities, drug dosages, vaccination dates or deworming intervals.
- Health reminders must be user-entered or veterinarian-confirmed; no automatic medical schedule is assumed.
- Urgent warning signs must direct the owner to a qualified veterinarian promptly.
- Separate sick/new animals where feasible and seek local veterinary advice on isolation and biosecurity.
- Keep animal profiles and health records private and on-device by default.
